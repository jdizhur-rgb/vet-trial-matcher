Phase II trametinib for dogs with histiocytic sarcoma

University of Florida · USA

Currently enrolling

Treatment: Multicenter Phase II trametinib study. Published recruitment criteria require a measurable tumor, no concurrent anticancer therapy, at least a 2-week washout after chemotherapy, and at least a 4-week washout after radiation therapy.

Key eligibility: confirmed: True; active_treatment_target: True; measurable: True; chemo_washout_days: 14; radiation_washout_days: 28

Important exclusions: current_chemo: True; current_radiation: True

Funding / cost: See official study page.

Contact: UF Clinical Studies — complete the Study Interest Form on the official trial page; UF Oncology Staff: (352) 392-2235

Quasiorganelle therapy for canine histiocytic sarcoma

Michigan State University Veterinary Medical Center · USA

Actively recruiting — current MSU clinical-trials page and August 24, 2026 recruitment announcement

Treatment: First cohort receives weakened non-disease-causing Listeria-derived quasiorganelle therapy alone; second cohort receives quasiorganelle therapy including gene therapy. Up to 5 visible lesions are allowed with summed longest dimensions under 15 cm. Weekly treatment x4, then 2 weeks off, repeated for a total 16-week treatment period. While enrolled, dogs should not live with someone pregnant or planning pregnancy.

Key eligibility: confirmed: True; active_treatment_target: True; measurable: True; visible_tumor: True; min_weight_kg: 20; max_vcog_performance_status: 2

Important exclusions: active_wounds: True; immunodeficiency: True; immune_mediated_disease: True; recent_systemic_antibiotics_14d: True; recent_immunosuppressants_14d: True; recent_prednisone_7d: True; recent_chemotherapy_14d: True; recent_radiation_30d: True

Funding / cost: Funded trial. Trial-associated tests, quasiorganelle treatment, hospitalization, blood/fecal sampling, imaging and treatment of trial-related complications are covered after enrollment; initial evaluation is charged.

Contact: MSU Medical Oncology Team — (517) 353-5420; cvm.oncoinfo@msu.edu

COTC033 repurposing vaccine immunity to treat canine cancers

Colorado State University Flint Animal Cancer Center · USA

Actively recruiting — listed on CSU current clinical trials page

Treatment: COTC033 current CSU treatment study. Published eligibility includes confirmed eligible cancer, a 2–8 cm tumor accessible for biopsy, and body weight 15–60 kg (33–132 lb). Structured criteria are applied by the global matcher; any known hard failure excludes the study.

Key eligibility: confirmed: True; active_treatment_target: True; measurable: True; min_weight_kg: 15; max_weight_kg: 60

Funding / cost: Confirm current study-covered and owner-paid costs with CSU Clinical Trials.

Contact: CSU Flint Animal Cancer Center Clinical Trials — (970) 297-4001; online clinical-trial consult form

Palbociclib for selected progressive canine solid tumors

Auburn University College of Veterinary Medicine · USA

Actively recruiting — confirmed on the institution’s current clinical-trials page

Treatment: Dose-finding oral palbociclib treatment study for measurable progressive mammary carcinoma, SCC, histiocytic sarcoma, AGASACA or melanoma; prior cancer treatment is allowed if disease is progressing.

Key eligibility: confirmed: True; active_treatment_target: True; measurable: True

Funding / cost: After owner-paid initial exam/screening, up to two months of trial treatment including biopsy, rechecks, bloodwork and chemotherapy are funded.

Contact: See official study page for current study contact and prescreening instructions
