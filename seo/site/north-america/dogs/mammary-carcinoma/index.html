Mammary Carcinoma: Clinical Trials and Cancer Treatment Studies for Dogs in USA & Canada

About Mammary Carcinoma

Mammary carcinoma is a malignant tumor of mammary tissue. Risk and behavior vary by species, tumor size, histologic features, lymph-node involvement and metastatic status; feline mammary carcinomas are often biologically aggressive.

Standard treatment

Surgery is the principal treatment for localized mammary carcinoma. The extent of surgery differs between dogs and cats and by distribution of disease. Chemotherapy may be considered for high-risk, node-positive or metastatic tumors, particularly in cats, based on pathology and stage.
