CARE trial: autophagy inhibition plus radiation for canine glioma

UC Davis Weill School of Veterinary Medicine / Translational Brain Tumor Center · USA

Actively recruiting — UC Davis recruitment announcement published September 3, 2026

Treatment: Combined Autophagy inhibition and Radiation to improve Efficacy in canine glioma (CARE). Dogs receive an experimental IV autophagy-inhibiting drug twice weekly for four weeks concurrently with standard radiation therapy, with brain-tumor biopsy before and after treatment. Current UC Davis materials state dogs with MRI-suspected glioma may be screened; final eligibility is determined by the neuro-oncology team.

Key eligibility: active_treatment_target: True; suspected_or_confirmed_glioma: True; radiation_therapy: True; brain_tumor_biopsy: True

Funding / cost: Study covers all study procedures and provides $4,000 toward radiation therapy; owners remain responsible for radiation costs beyond the study contribution.

Contact: UC Davis Veterinary Center for Clinical Trials — (530) 754-1954; contact Dr. Christine Toedebusch / study team via the official UC Davis page

Radiation therapy plus temozolomide for canine brain tumors

Johns Hopkins Center for Image-Guided Animal Therapy (CIGAT) · USA

Open and enrolling — current Johns Hopkins CIGAT clinical-trials page

Treatment: Treatment study for dogs with a suspected glioma/intra-axial brain tumor greater than 1 cm on MRI and restricted to one side of the brain. Protocol uses five consecutive workdays of radiation therapy with concurrent oral temozolomide. Previous or current oncologic treatment of the brain tumor is exclusionary; investigator prescreening is required.

Key eligibility: active_treatment_target: True; min_age_years: 1; min_weight_kg: 10; brain_tumor_present: True

Important exclusions: prior_local_radiation: True; prior_chemo: True

Funding / cost: Radiation therapy and chemotherapy are fully funded. Study-related medications for side effects, follow-up MRI, and blood tests are also covered; treatment after progression is not covered.

Contact: Johns Hopkins CIGAT — 410-502-7325; contact through the official clinical-trials page
