Thyroid Carcinoma: Clinical Trials and Cancer Treatment Studies for Dogs in USA & Canada

About Thyroid Carcinoma

Thyroid carcinoma can occur as a movable, surgically approachable neck mass or as an invasive tumor involving surrounding structures. Functional status and metastatic risk vary.

Standard treatment

Surgery is preferred for resectable, mobile thyroid tumors. Radiation is an important option for invasive or incompletely resectable disease. Radioactive iodine may be appropriate for selected iodine-avid tumors, while systemic therapy can be considered for metastatic or progressive disease.

Histotripsy for canine thyroid tumors

Virginia-Maryland College of Veterinary Medicine / Virginia Tech · USA

Recruiting — listed on Virginia Tech current studies page

Treatment: Non-invasive histotripsy intended to precisely destroy canine thyroid tumor tissue without incisions or radiation. Full protocol eligibility requires investigator prescreening.

Key eligibility: Active tumor required; Thyroid tumor

Costs: Confirm study-covered and owner-paid costs with the study team.
