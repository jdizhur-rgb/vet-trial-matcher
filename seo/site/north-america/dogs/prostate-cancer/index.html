Prostate Cancer: Clinical Trials and Cancer Treatment Studies for Dogs in USA & Canada

About Prostate Cancer

Prostatic carcinoma is an aggressive cancer in dogs that can invade the urinary tract and metastasize to lymph nodes, lungs and bone. Complete surgical removal is often difficult because of anatomy and disease behavior.

Standard treatment

Treatment is usually multimodal and may include NSAIDs, chemotherapy, radiation and procedures to relieve urinary obstruction. Surgery is reserved for selected cases. Choice of therapy depends heavily on local extent, urinary function and metastatic disease.

Z-007 immune therapy for dogs with solid tumors

Recruiting

Treatment: Recruiting dogs with biopsy-accessible solid tumors generally 2–7 cm. Prior surgery, chemotherapy or radiation may be allowed; prior immunotherapy is excluded.
