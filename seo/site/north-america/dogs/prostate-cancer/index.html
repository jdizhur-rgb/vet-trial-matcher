Tufts University · USA

Recruiting

PSMA-MMAE-Pc413 theranostic plus laser for canine prostatic cancer

University of Pennsylvania School of Veterinary Medicine · USA

Current Penn Vet clinical trial

Treatment: Targeted investigational PSMA ligand carrying MMAE and a photosensitizer, combined with laser photodynamic therapy; intended for local and metastatic canine prostatic cancer.

Key eligibility: confirmed: True; active_treatment_target: True; prostatic_cancer: True

Funding / cost: Study covers specified diagnostics after enrollment, theranostic infusions, laser, hospitalization and study rechecks including imaging and laboratory testing.
