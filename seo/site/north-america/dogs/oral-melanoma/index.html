Oral Melanoma: Clinical Trials and Cancer Treatment Studies for Dogs in USA & Canada

About Oral Melanoma

Oral melanoma is an aggressive oral cancer in dogs with significant risks of local invasion and spread to regional lymph nodes and lungs. Biological behavior can vary, but staging is important before treatment planning.

Standard treatment

Surgery is preferred when the tumor can be removed with acceptable function. Radiation is commonly used for tumors that cannot be completely excised or when surgery is not feasible. Systemic and immunotherapeutic approaches may be considered because metastatic risk is substantial, although benefit varies by patient and disease stage.
