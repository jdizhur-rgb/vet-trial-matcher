Z-007 immune therapy for dogs with solid tumors

Tufts University · USA

Recruiting

Treatment: Recruiting dogs with biopsy-accessible solid tumors generally 2–7 cm. Prior surgery, chemotherapy or radiation may be allowed; prior immunotherapy is excluded.

Intravesical IL-12 immunotherapy for canine invasive bladder cancer

NC State College of Veterinary Medicine · USA

Actively recruiting — enrollment finish September 30, 2026

Treatment: Experimental intravesical IL-12 immunotherapy. Metastatic UCC is permitted. Untreated and previously treated dogs may qualify; at least one month is required since last radiation or chemotherapy. No placebo group; all enrolled dogs receive immunotherapy.

Key eligibility: confirmed: True; active_treatment_target: True; min_weight_kg: 8; muscle_invasive_bladder_mass: True

Important exclusions: current_prednisone: True; prior_immunotherapy: True

Funding / cost: Pre-staging, IL-12 immunotherapy, hospitalization, anesthesia and study monitoring are provided at no cost.

Contact: NC State Clinical Studies Core — see official trial page

Purdue University Veterinary Hospital / Werling Comparative Oncology Research Center · USA

Enrollment ongoing in 2026

Treatment: NCI-funded Purdue-directed exploratory treatment trial of the caninized anti-PD-L1 monoclonal antibody AKS-701d in client-owned dogs with histopathologically confirmed invasive urothelial carcinoma. Public 2026 development disclosures state enrollment is ongoing; Purdue's current cancer-research page identifies the anti-cPD-L1 bladder-cancer trial as active research.
