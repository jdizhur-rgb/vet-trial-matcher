Squamous Cell Carcinoma: Clinical Trials and Cancer Treatment Studies for Dogs in USA & Canada

About Squamous Cell Carcinoma

Squamous cell carcinoma is a malignant tumor of squamous epithelial cells. Behavior varies greatly by site, from some relatively controllable skin lesions to invasive oral, nasal or other tumors.

Standard treatment

Local treatment is selected by site and extent and may include surgery, radiation or other local therapies. Systemic treatment is considered for selected advanced or metastatic cases. Site-specific staging and pathology are important because treatment and prognosis differ substantially.

Z-007 immune therapy for dogs with solid tumors

Tufts University · USA

Recruiting

Treatment: Recruiting dogs with biopsy-accessible solid tumors generally 2–7 cm. Prior surgery, chemotherapy or radiation may be allowed; prior immunotherapy is excluded.

Key eligibility: Confirmed; Measurable; Min weight lb: 15.4; Measurable or lung metastasis; Min weight kg: 7

Important exclusions: Prior immunotherapy

Costs: Study cancer-treatment costs covered through Day 63, including treatment of study-related side effects.
