Z-007 immune therapy for dogs with solid tumors

Tufts University · USA

Recruiting

Treatment: Recruiting dogs with biopsy-accessible solid tumors generally 2–7 cm. Prior surgery, chemotherapy or radiation may be allowed; prior immunotherapy is excluded.

Key eligibility: confirmed: True; measurable: True; min_weight_lb: 15.4; measurable_or_lung_metastasis: True; min_weight_kg: 7

Important exclusions: prior_immunotherapy: True

Funding / cost: Study cancer-treatment costs covered through Day 63, including treatment of study-related side effects.

Contact: Tufts Clinical Trials Office — clinicaltrials@tufts.edu

Palbociclib for selected progressive canine solid tumors

Auburn University College of Veterinary Medicine · USA

Actively recruiting — confirmed on the institution’s current clinical-trials page

Treatment: Dose-finding oral palbociclib treatment study for measurable progressive mammary carcinoma, SCC, histiocytic sarcoma, AGASACA or melanoma; prior cancer treatment is allowed if disease is progressing.

Key eligibility: confirmed: True; active_treatment_target: True; measurable: True

Funding / cost: After owner-paid initial exam/screening, up to two months of trial treatment including biopsy, rechecks, bloodwork and chemotherapy are funded.
