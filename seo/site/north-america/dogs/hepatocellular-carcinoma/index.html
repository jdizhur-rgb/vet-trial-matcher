Hepatocellular Carcinoma: Clinical Trials and Cancer Treatment Studies for Dogs in USA & Canada

About Hepatocellular Carcinoma

Hepatocellular carcinoma is a primary liver cancer. A solitary massive form can behave very differently from diffuse or nodular disease, so imaging pattern and resectability strongly influence prognosis.

Standard treatment

Surgical removal is the preferred treatment for a resectable solitary massive hepatocellular carcinoma and can provide prolonged control. Diffuse, multifocal, unresectable or metastatic disease requires individualized management, and systemic or investigational options may be considered.
