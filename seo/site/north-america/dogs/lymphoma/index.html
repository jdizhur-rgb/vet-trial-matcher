Aurelius/Lytix oncolytic peptide plus adoptive T-cell immunotherapy for canine B-cell lymphoma

Aurelius Biotherapeutics · USA

Actively recruiting — confirmed on Aurelius current pilot-study page

Treatment: Initial pilot study combining an oncolytic peptide with adoptive T-cell immunotherapy for previously untreated stage 3–5(a) canine B-cell lymphoma. Almost all study treatment is performed in Bellingham, Washington; initial treatment requires about three days, followed by at least two later visits approximately a month afterward.

Key eligibility: confirmed: True; active_treatment_target: True; previously_untreated: True

Important exclusions: prior_cancer_treatment: True

Funding / cost: Fully funded; Aurelius states there is no additional expense to owners for the trial.

Contact: Theresa Westfall, DVM — info@aureliusbio.com; (360) 961-3529 or (360) 734-0720

Fully funded CAR-T cell therapy trial for canine B-cell lymphoma

LEAH Labs / University of Minnesota / Ohio State University / University of Missouri · USA

Trial open — actively enrolling at three partner institutions

Treatment: Active therapeutic CAR-T cell trial for dogs diagnosed with B-cell lymphoma. LEAH Labs currently lists three actively enrolling sites: University of Minnesota College of Veterinary Medicine (Minneapolis, MN), Ohio State University College of Veterinary Medicine (Columbus, OH), and University of Missouri Veterinary Health Center (Columbia, MO). Detailed owner-facing inclusion/exclusion criteria are not published on the sponsor page, so investigator prescreening is required.

Key eligibility: confirmed: True; active_treatment_target: True

Funding / cost: LEAH Labs states the trial is fully funded and covers 100% of costs for enrolled families.

Contact: LEAH Labs — info@leahlabs.com; enrollment request form on the official site
