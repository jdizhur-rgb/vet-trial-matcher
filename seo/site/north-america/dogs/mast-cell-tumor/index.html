Mast Cell Tumor: Clinical Trials and Cancer Treatment Studies for Dogs in USA & Canada

About Mast Cell Tumor

Mast cell tumors arise from mast cells and range from relatively indolent skin tumors to aggressive cancers with metastatic potential. Prognosis depends on grade, location, surgical margins, proliferation markers and evidence of spread.

Standard treatment

Surgery with appropriate margins is the primary treatment for most localized cutaneous mast cell tumors. Radiation can provide local control when margins are incomplete and further surgery is not feasible. Higher-risk, unresectable or metastatic disease may require systemic therapy, including chemotherapy or targeted tyrosine-kinase inhibitors, depending on tumor biology and clinical circumstances.
