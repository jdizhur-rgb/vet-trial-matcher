Current recruiting clinical study

Treatment: Easily accessible STS >=2 cm and suitable surgical candidate. Hydrogel + T-cell engager injection 7 days before surgery. Study pays $1,000 toward WSU bill plus ICU, study-related medications and study bloodwork; owner pays standard-of-care surgery/diagnostics.

Funding / cost: VRCCO: $1,500 toward tumor-removal surgery plus ICU, study-related bloodwork and medications. WSU: $1,000 medical credit plus ICU/study-related medications and bloodwork.
