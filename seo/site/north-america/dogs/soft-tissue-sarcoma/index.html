Soft Tissue Sarcoma: Clinical Trials and Cancer Treatment Studies for Dogs in USA & Canada

About Soft Tissue Sarcoma

Soft tissue sarcomas are a diverse group of tumors arising from connective and supporting tissues. They tend to invade locally; metastatic risk varies with histologic subtype and grade.

Standard treatment

Wide surgical excision is the principal treatment for resectable soft tissue sarcoma. Radiation is commonly considered for incomplete or narrow margins when additional surgery is impractical. Chemotherapy may be discussed for high-grade, metastatic or selected high-risk tumors, but its role varies by subtype and patient.
