CAR T cell immunotherapy for treatment of metastatic osteosarcoma in dogs

Colorado State University Flint Animal Cancer Center · USA

Actively recruiting — confirmed on CSU current clinical trials page and August 4, 2026 recruitment post

Treatment: Confirmed osteosarcoma after primary-tumor surgery with measurable pulmonary metastases on chest radiographs. Requires adequate bloodwork and 2–3 week chemotherapy washout. Protocol includes lymphodepleting fludarabine/cyclophosphamide, IV CAR T-cell infusion, plus oral losartan and propranolol. All study visits occur at CSU.

Key eligibility: confirmed: True; active_treatment_target: True; primary_removed: True; measurable_lung_metastases: True; min_weight_kg: 20

Important exclusions: metastases_outside_lungs: True; concurrent_immunomodulatory_drugs: True; corticosteroids: True

Funding / cost: Once enrolled, study covers clinical-trial costs including diagnostics, CAR T-cell generation and infusion, and study blood collection/processing, except recheck exam fees. Screening funds and up to $2,000 for treatment of trial-related side effects are available.

Contact: CSU Flint Animal Cancer Center Clinical Trials — (970) 297-4001; online clinical-trial consult form

Cryotherapy or intratumoral immunotherapy before amputation for canine osteosarcoma

Johns Hopkins Center for Image-Guided Animal Therapy (CIGAT) · USA

Open and recruiting — listed on the current Johns Hopkins CIGAT clinical-trials page

Treatment: Treatment trial for histopathologically confirmed canine osteosarcoma. Eligible dogs receive PET-CT and either cryotherapy or two doses of tumor-injected immunotherapy before required amputation at about two weeks, followed by chemotherapy. Johns Hopkins lists no prior osteosarcoma treatment or chemotherapy for other cancers and requires the dog to be systemically healthy apart from osteosarcoma.

Key eligibility: confirmed: True; active_treatment_target: True; planned_amputation_and_chemo: True

Important exclusions: prior_chemo: True; prior_osteosarcoma_treatment: True

Funding / cost: Study covers PET-CT/CT imaging, cryotherapy or intratumoral immunotherapy, study-related medications, and follow-up imaging/blood tests. Required amputation and chemotherapy are not covered by the study.

Contact: Johns Hopkins CIGAT — 410-502-7325; contact through the official clinical-trials page
