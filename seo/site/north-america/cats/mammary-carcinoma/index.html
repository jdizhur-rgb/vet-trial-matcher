Novel anticancer therapy for metastatic feline mammary carcinoma

Michigan State University · USA

Current oncology clinical trial

Treatment: Metastatic mammary carcinoma required; prior mastectomy and prior chemotherapy may be allowed; concurrent NSAIDs allowed; repeated imaging/treatments and follow-up may continue up to 12 months.

Key eligibility: confirmed: True; metastatic: True; measurable: True

Funding / cost: Investigational miR-10b therapeutic and associated MRI/CT imaging are funded; unrelated diagnostics/treatment owner-paid.

Contact: MSU Veterinary Medical Center Oncology — 517-353-5420

University of Minnesota Veterinary Medical Center · USA

Actively recruiting — confirmed on the institution’s current clinical-trials page
