Oral Squamous Cell Carcinoma: Clinical Trials and Cancer Treatment Studies for Cats in USA & Canada

About Oral Squamous Cell Carcinoma

Oral squamous cell carcinoma is a locally invasive epithelial cancer of the mouth. In cats it is particularly aggressive locally and is often diagnosed when complete surgical removal is difficult.

Standard treatment

Surgery offers the best chance of durable local control when complete excision is anatomically possible. Radiation, including definitive or palliative approaches, may be considered for unresectable or incompletely excised tumors. Systemic therapies have limited and variable benefit, which is why new local and targeted treatments are actively studied.
