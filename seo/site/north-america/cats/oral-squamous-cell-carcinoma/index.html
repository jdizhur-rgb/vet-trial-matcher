Funded active-study evidence; enrollment should be confirmed with Illinois

PIVOT-C intratumoral FVRCP vaccine therapy for feline oral tumors

NC State College of Veterinary Medicine · USA

Actively recruiting — enrollment finish December 31, 2027

Treatment: 28-day pilot treatment study with weekly intratumoral FVRCP vaccine injections on days 0, 7, 14, 21 and 28. Cats cannot receive surgery or radiation while enrolled; those treatments may be considered after study completion or early exit.

Key eligibility: active_treatment_target: True; oral_mass: True

Important exclusions: concurrent_surgery: True; concurrent_local_radiation: True

Funding / cost: Confirm study-covered and owner-paid costs with the investigator.

Contact: Dr. Joanne Intile / NC State — see official trial page
