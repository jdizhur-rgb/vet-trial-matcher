Lymphoma: Clinical Trials and Cancer Treatment Studies for Cats in USA & Canada

About Lymphoma

Lymphoma is a cancer of lymphocytes and can involve lymph nodes, blood, spleen, liver, gastrointestinal tract, skin or other organs. Behavior and prognosis vary substantially by anatomic form and immunophenotype, especially B-cell versus T-cell disease.

Standard treatment

For most dogs with high-grade multicentric lymphoma, multi-agent chemotherapy such as a CHOP-based protocol is the standard first-line approach. Cats are treated according to lymphoma type and site; multi-agent chemotherapy is common for high-grade disease, while some low-grade gastrointestinal lymphomas are managed with oral chemotherapy and corticosteroids. Radiation or surgery may have roles in selected localized forms.
