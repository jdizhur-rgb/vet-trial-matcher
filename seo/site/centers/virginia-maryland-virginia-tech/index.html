About Virginia Tech Veterinary Clinical Research

Virginia Tech's Veterinary Clinical Research Office facilitates clinical trials and translational studies at the Virginia-Maryland College of Veterinary Medicine and Veterinary Teaching Hospital, including oncology research for companion animals.

The program lists oncology among its research specialties and works with veterinary investigators, research staff, sponsors and pet owners to test new approaches to diagnosis and treatment.

Histotripsy for canine thyroid tumors

Virginia-Maryland College of Veterinary Medicine / Virginia Tech · USA

Recruiting — listed on Virginia Tech current studies page

What is being offered: Non-invasive histotripsy intended to precisely destroy canine thyroid tumor tissue without incisions or radiation. Full protocol eligibility requires investigator prescreening.

Who may qualify: Active tumor required; Thyroid tumor
