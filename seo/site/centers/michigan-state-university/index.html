About MSU Veterinary Oncology

Michigan State University Veterinary Medical Center combines clinical cancer care with oncology research in dogs and cats. Its oncologists conduct clinical studies designed to find safer or more effective ways to diagnose, prevent and treat cancer, with some studies also contributing to comparative cancer research.

MSU's current oncology portfolio includes studies in lymphoma, histiocytic sarcoma, mast cell tumors and other solid tumors. The medical oncology faculty include board-certified veterinary oncologists, including Kelly Hume, DVM, DACVIM (Oncology).

Quasiorganelle therapy for canine histiocytic sarcoma

Michigan State University Veterinary Medical Center · USA

Actively recruiting — current MSU clinical-trials page and August 24, 2026 recruitment announcement

What is being offered: First cohort receives weakened non-disease-causing Listeria-derived quasiorganelle therapy alone; second cohort receives quasiorganelle therapy including gene therapy. Up to 5 visible lesions are allowed with summed longest dimensions under 15 cm. Weekly treatment x4, then 2 weeks off, repeated for a total 16-week treatment period. While enrolled, dogs should not live with someone pregnant or planning pregnancy.

Who may qualify: Confirmed; Active tumor required; Measurable; Min weight kg: 20; Visible tumor; Max vcog performance status: 2

May not qualify if: Current chemo; Current steroids; Current radiation; Active wounds; Immunodeficiency; Immune mediated disease; Recent systemic antibiotics 14d; Recent immunosuppressants 14d; Recent prednisone 7d; Recent chemotherapy 14d; Recent radiation 30d

Costs / coverage: 🟢 Fully funded after enrollment. Trial-associated tests, quasiorganelle treatment, hospitalization, blood/fecal sampling, imaging and treatment of trial-related complications are covered; the initial evaluation is owner-paid.

Contact: MSU Medical Oncology Team — (517) 353-5420; cvm.oncoinfo@msu.edu

Novel anticancer therapy for metastatic feline mammary carcinoma

Michigan State University · USA

Current oncology clinical trial

What is being offered: Metastatic mammary carcinoma required; prior mastectomy and prior chemotherapy may be allowed; concurrent NSAIDs allowed; repeated imaging/treatments and follow-up may continue up to 12 months.

Who may qualify: Confirmed; Metastatic; Measurable

Costs / coverage: 🟡 Partially funded. Investigational miR-10b therapy and associated MRI/CT imaging are funded; unrelated diagnostics and treatment remain owner-paid.

Contact: MSU Veterinary Medical Center Oncology — 517-353-5420
