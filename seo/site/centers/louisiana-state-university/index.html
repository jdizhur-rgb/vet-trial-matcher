About LSU Veterinary Cancer Research

LSU Vet Med combines its Cancer Treatment Unit with the Center for Clinical Innovation and a growing comparative oncology research network. The school conducts veterinary cancer trials while building translational programs that connect naturally occurring animal cancers with laboratory and human-cancer research.

LSU's Center for Comparative Oncology is directed by Joseph Francis, BVSc, MVSc, PhD, who also leads the Center for Pre-Clinical Cancer Research. The Center for Clinical Innovation is directed by Levent Dirikolu and coordinates hospital trials, including a current intratumoral treatment study for canine squamous cell carcinoma.

Cancer treatment & research options

Intratumoral chemotherapy for non-metastatic canine squamous cell carcinoma

LSU Veterinary Teaching Hospital · USA

Actively recruiting — confirmed on the institution’s current clinical-trials page

What is being offered: Direct intratumoral chemotherapy for confirmed non-metastatic SCC; lesion must be greater than 1 cm; no prior radiation, immunotherapy or chemotherapy.

Who may qualify: Confirmed; Active tumor required; Min tumor cm: 1; Min weight kg: 5; No metastasis

May not qualify if: Prior chemo; Prior immunotherapy; Prior local radiation

Costs / coverage: Treatment is free and the study provides a $400 gift card upon completion.

Contact: See official study page for current study contact and prescreening instructions
