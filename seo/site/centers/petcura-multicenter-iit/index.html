Personalized mRNA-LNP immunotherapy for canine malignant tumors

PETcura (宝科雅) multicenter IIT · China

Recruiting — limited enrollment

What is being offered: Official study page updated 2026-08-15. Exploratory multicenter IIT for canine malignant tumors using fresh tumor plus paired adjacent normal tissue for >=50G RNA sequencing, neoantigen selection and personalized mRNA-LNP immunotherapy. T-cell lymphoma and brain tumors are explicitly excluded and therefore are not mapped to this record. Requires histologic or cytologic confirmation, age >=2 years, generally weight >=5 kg (smaller dogs may be individually assessed), fresh tumor and paired normal tissue obtainable at surgery; dogs already operated may still be considered if tissue was correctly frozen. Also requires adequate performance status/labs and successful LNP tolerance testing. Excludes recent chemotherapy/radiation/high-dose corticosteroids within 2 weeks, severe infection/autoimmune disease/immunodeficiency, pregnancy/lactation, and expected survival <6 months. Owners pay some sampling/freezing/sequencing costs; personalized mRNA design/manufacture is funded by PETcura.

Who may qualify: Confirmed; Min age years: 2; Min weight kg: 5

Contact: WeChat: petcura; project team matches eligible owners with a participating veterinarian/hospital.
