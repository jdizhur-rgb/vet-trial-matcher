About Illinois Veterinary Oncology

The University of Illinois Cancer Care Clinic combines specialty cancer care with an active comparative oncology and clinical trials program. The Veterinary Teaching Hospital works with research collaborators to evaluate novel anticancer drugs and treatment strategies in dogs and cats.

The oncology clinical trials team includes Timothy Fan, DVM, PhD, Matthew Berry, DVM, PhD, radiation oncologist Kimberly Selting, DVM, MS, Joanna Schmit, DVM, MS, and clinical trials manager Rebecca Kamerer, CVT. Research includes cancer vaccines, immunotherapy, radiation combinations, cellular therapy and novel drug delivery.
