CAR T cell immunotherapy for treatment of metastatic osteosarcoma in dogs

Colorado State University Flint Animal Cancer Center · USA

Actively recruiting — confirmed on CSU current clinical trials page and August 4, 2026 recruitment post

What is being offered: Confirmed osteosarcoma after primary-tumor surgery with measurable pulmonary metastases on chest radiographs. Requires adequate bloodwork and 2–3 week chemotherapy washout. Protocol includes lymphodepleting fludarabine/cyclophosphamide, IV CAR T-cell infusion, plus oral losartan and propranolol. All study visits occur at CSU.

Who may qualify: Confirmed; Metastatic; Active tumor required; Primary removed; Measurable lung metastases; Min weight kg: 20

May not qualify if: Metastases outside lungs; Concurrent immunomodulatory drugs; Corticosteroids

Costs / coverage: 🟡 **Partially funded** — AVMA lists expected owner cost under $500; the study record also describes covered trial diagnostics, CAR T-cell generation/infusion and study blood processing.

Contact: CSU Flint Animal Cancer Center Clinical Trials — (970) 297-4001; online clinical-trial consult form

Location

Intravenous carboplatin for pain control in canine appendicular osteosarcoma

Colorado State University Flint Animal Cancer Center · USA

Actively recruiting — listed on CSU current clinical trials page

What is being offered: Single IV carboplatin treatment followed by three weeks of activity, gait, imaging and laboratory monitoring. Requires seven days on a standard oral pain regimen before enrollment and a 30-day bisphosphonate washout.

Who may qualify: Confirmed; Active tumor required; Appendicular location; Weight bearing

May not qualify if: Prior chemo; Prior radiation; Bone metastasis

Costs / coverage: Study covers pre-enrollment CBC, chemistry, urinalysis, chest and limb radiographs and tumor aspirate/biopsy; after enrollment it covers one carboplatin treatment, study rechecks, repeat bloodwork and limb radiographs, plus initial anti-nausea/anti-diarrheal medications.

Contact: CSU Flint Animal Cancer Center Clinical Trials — (970) 297-4001; online clinical-trial consult form

Location

  • Flint Animal Cancer Center, 300 W Drake Rd, Fort Collins, CO 80523
