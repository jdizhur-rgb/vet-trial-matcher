Lattice or SBRT radiotherapy for large inoperable canine tumors

University of Zurich — Division of Radiation Oncology · Switzerland

Ongoing European oncology clinical trial — listed by Oncowaf

What is being offered: Therapeutic radiotherapy trial using lattice RT or SBRT for large inoperable tumors, or tumors for which radiotherapy is preferred to surgery. Tumor-specific screening is required.

Who may qualify: Confirmed; Active tumor required; Large inoperable or rt preferred

Costs / coverage: Confirm costs with University of Zurich.

Contact: University of Zurich Radiation Oncology — +41 44 635 81 12

Fluorescence-guided surgery for canine peripheral nerve sheath / plexus tumors

Universitäres Tierspital Zürich / University of Zurich · Switzerland

Studienteilnahme möglich

What is being offered: Therapeutic surgical study for peripheral nerve sheath/plexus tumors using fluorescence guidance to improve tumor visualization and potentially enable limb-preserving surgery. PNST-specific; other STS are not automatically eligible.

Who may qualify: Confirmed; Active tumor required

Costs / coverage: Confirm study-covered and owner-paid costs with the University of Zurich study team.

Contact: University of Zurich study team — use official study page
