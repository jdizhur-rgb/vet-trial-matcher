SOLID-Relapse — treatment study for dogs with relapsed multicentric nodal lymphoma

Colorado Animal Specialty & Emergency (CASE) / Ethos Discovery · USA

Currently enrolling

What is being offered: Randomized lymphoma treatment under an Ethos oncologist plus genomic/biomarker sampling

Who may qualify: Confirmed; Prior chemo; Active tumor required

Costs / coverage: Full screening; scheduled bloodwork/urinalysis; up to $4,500 compensation for chemotherapy, exams, diagnostics and medications for chemotherapy-related side effects.

Contact: CASE Clinical Trials — CASEtrials@ethosvet.com; 303-545-2273

Ethos-PUSH precision-medicine umbrella study for splenic hemangiosarcoma

Ethos Discovery / multicenter specialty hospitals · USA

Open / currently enrolling

What is being offered: Phase 1 splenectomy/hemoabdomen study; dogs confirmed with non-metastatic splenic HSA can continue to fully funded active-treatment Phase 2, with treatment assigned among doxorubicin, doxorubicin+rapamycin, doxorubicin+sorafenib, or oral paclitaxel regimens.

Who may qualify: Min weight lb: 11.0; No metastasis; Max weight lb: 99.2; Active tumor required

Costs / coverage: `Fully funded` After initial screening, per AVMA Veterinary Clinical Trials Registry.

Contact: Ethos Discovery enrollment form / participating hospital; CASE: 303-545-2273
