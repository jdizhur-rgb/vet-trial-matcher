About Missouri Veterinary Oncology

The University of Missouri Veterinary Health Center supports a multidisciplinary oncology program spanning medical, surgical and radiation oncology, with clinical research integrated into specialty care. Cancer patients may receive conventional care as well as investigational therapies through the college's research program.

Missouri's oncology program includes board-certified medical, radiation and surgical oncologists and clinical-trial support, with research collaborations across veterinary and human cancer disciplines.

Timing of carboplatin chemotherapy after amputation for canine osteosarcoma

University of Missouri Veterinary Health Center · USA

Currently enrolling

What is being offered: Current live Missouri oncology roster explicitly lists 'Evaluation of Timing of Carboplatin Chemotherapy post Amputation for Osteosarcoma.' Dogs undergo amputation and begin carboplatin the day after surgery or immediately before hospital discharge, then are followed for metastasis. This is an interventional timing study, not merely observational.

Fully funded CAR-T cell therapy trial for canine B-cell lymphoma

LEAH Labs / University of Minnesota / Ohio State University / University of Missouri · USA

Trial open — actively enrolling at three partner institutions

What is being offered: Active therapeutic CAR-T cell trial for dogs diagnosed with B-cell lymphoma. LEAH Labs currently lists three actively enrolling sites: University of Minnesota College of Veterinary Medicine (Minneapolis, MN), Ohio State University College of Veterinary Medicine (Columbus, OH), and University of Missouri Veterinary Health Center (Columbia, MO). Detailed owner-facing inclusion/exclusion criteria are not published on the sponsor page, so investigator prescreening is required.

Who may qualify: Confirmed; Active tumor required

Costs / coverage: LEAH Labs states the trial is fully funded and covers 100% of costs for enrolled families.

Contact: LEAH Labs — info@leahlabs.com; enrollment request form on the official site
