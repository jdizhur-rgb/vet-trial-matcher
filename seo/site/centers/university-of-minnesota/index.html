About Minnesota Animal Cancer Research

The University of Minnesota Animal Cancer Care and Research Program links the Veterinary Medical Center, Clinical Investigation Center and Masonic Cancer Center to move discoveries from laboratory cancer biology into clinical studies for companion animals.

The program studies cancer causes, behavior, prevention and treatment. The Modiano Lab is a major canine comparative oncology group, while clinical investigators such as Amber Wolf-Ringwall work in hematologic malignancies, cancer biology and translational oncology. Current studies span lymphoma, osteosarcoma, melanoma and feline mammary carcinoma.

Open and enrolling

TriKE immunotherapy plus hypofractionated radiation for canine sarcoma

University of Minnesota College of Veterinary Medicine · USA

Open and enrolling — confirmed on University of Minnesota current clinical-trial page

What is being offered: Dose-finding treatment study of TriKE immunotherapy with hypofractionated radiation in companion dogs with sarcoma. Local/regional lymph-node involvement may be accepted at investigator discretion; prior chemotherapy or radiation may be allowed after washout.

Who may qualify: Confirmed; Active tumor required; Measurable; Min tumor cm: 2; Min weight kg: 7; No metastasis

May not qualify if: Current chemo; Current radiation; Prior immunotherapy

Costs / coverage: Once enrolled, the study covers participation costs including blood/urine testing, imaging, radiation planning and treatment, TriKE immunotherapy and follow-up exams. Initial staging is also covered if performed at the Veterinary Medical Center.

Contact: Amber Winter, CVT — 612-624-1352; contact through the official University of Minnesota study page

Fully funded CAR-T cell therapy trial for canine B-cell lymphoma

LEAH Labs / University of Minnesota / Ohio State University / University of Missouri · USA

Trial open — actively enrolling at three partner institutions

What is being offered: Active therapeutic CAR-T cell trial for dogs diagnosed with B-cell lymphoma. LEAH Labs currently lists three actively enrolling sites: University of Minnesota College of Veterinary Medicine (Minneapolis, MN), Ohio State University College of Veterinary Medicine (Columbus, OH), and University of Missouri Veterinary Health Center (Columbia, MO). Detailed owner-facing inclusion/exclusion criteria are not published on the sponsor page, so investigator prescreening is required.

Who may qualify: Confirmed; Active tumor required

Costs / coverage: LEAH Labs states the trial is fully funded and covers 100% of costs for enrolled families.

Contact: LEAH Labs — info@leahlabs.com; enrollment request form on the official site
