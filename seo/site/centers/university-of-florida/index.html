About UF Veterinary Oncology

The University of Florida Veterinary Hospitals run a dedicated clinical studies program with active medical and radiation oncology studies. The program connects naturally occurring cancers in dogs and cats with clinical and translational research aimed at improving diagnosis and treatment.

Current oncology research includes cancer vaccines, immunotherapy, targeted therapy and radiation oncology. Rowan Milner, BVSc, heads the clinical studies team as Director of Clinical and Translational Research; the oncology clinical research faculty include Kelly Deabold, DVM, and Kelly Harrison, DVM.

Phase II trametinib for dogs with histiocytic sarcoma

University of Florida · USA

Currently enrolling

What is being offered: Multicenter Phase II trametinib study. Published recruitment criteria require a measurable tumor, no concurrent anticancer therapy, at least a 2-week washout after chemotherapy, and at least a 4-week washout after radiation therapy.

Who may qualify: Confirmed; Active tumor required; Measurable; chemotherapy washout: 14 days; radiation washout: 28 days

May not qualify if: Current chemo; Current radiation

Costs / coverage: 🟡 **Partially funded** — AVMA lists expected owner cost between $501 and $1,000.

Contact: UF Clinical Studies — complete the Study Interest Form on the official trial page; UF Oncology Staff: (352) 392-2235
