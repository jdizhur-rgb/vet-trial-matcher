About Tufts Veterinary Oncology

Tufts' Harrington Oncology Service at Foster Hospital brings medical oncology, radiation oncology and clinical trials together for dogs and cats. The Clinical Trials Office supports studies of new treatments, diagnostics and devices and routinely enrolls veterinary patients in oncology research.

The Clinical Trials Office is directed by veterinary oncologist Cheryl London, DVM, PhD. Current Tufts cancer research includes immunotherapy, CAR-T approaches, osteosarcoma, melanoma, soft tissue sarcoma and hemangiosarcoma studies.

Z-007 immune therapy for dogs with solid tumors

Tufts University · USA

Recruiting

What is being offered: Recruiting dogs with biopsy-accessible solid tumors generally 2–7 cm. Prior surgery, chemotherapy or radiation may be allowed; prior immunotherapy is excluded.

Who may qualify: Confirmed; Measurable; Min weight lb: 15.4; Measurable or lung metastasis; Min weight kg: 7

May not qualify if: Prior immunotherapy

Costs / coverage: 🟢 Fully funded study treatment. Study cancer-treatment costs are covered through Day 63, including treatment of study-related side effects.

Contact: Tufts Clinical Trials Office — clinicaltrials@tufts.edu

Liquid-biopsy guided treatment study for splenic hemangiosarcoma

Tufts University · USA

Recruiting

What is being offered: Splenic hemangiosarcoma without obvious spread; dogs cannot have received prior HSA treatment. Recent splenectomy elsewhere may be accepted after pathology confirmation.

Who may qualify: Confirmed; No metastasis; Min weight lb: 22; Active tumor required; Min weight kg: 10; Post splenectomy

Costs / coverage: 🟡 Partially funded. The study provides up to $5,000 toward study treatment and supplies the study oral inhibitors at no cost.

Contact: Tufts Clinical Trials Office — clinicaltrials@tufts.edu

Evaluation of TLR agonist Z-007 in dogs with cancer

Cummings School of Veterinary Medicine, Tufts University · USA

Recruiting

What is being offered: Dog study of IV or intratumoral Z-007 for solid tumors including carcinomas, sarcomas and melanoma. Public criteria include a generally 2–7 cm repeat-biopsy-accessible tumor plus medical/laboratory requirements; no prior immunotherapy or autoimmune disease. Investigator prescreening required.

Who may qualify: Confirmed; Min weight kg: 7; Measurable

May not qualify if: Prior immunotherapy

Costs / coverage: 🟡 Partially funded. Z-007 treatment through Day 63 and treatment of Z-007/biopsy-related side effects are covered; other owner costs should be confirmed with Tufts.

Contact: clinicaltrials@tufts.edu

Tumor-microenvironment modification plus allogeneic vaccine for metastatic canine osteosarcoma

Tufts Cummings School of Veterinary Medicine · USA

Actively recruiting — confirmed on the institution’s current clinical-trials page

What is being offered: For dogs with pulmonary metastatic osteosarcoma after amputation and chemotherapy; combines toceranib, losartan and ladarixin with a tumor vaccine.

Who may qualify: Confirmed; Active tumor required; Primary removed; Measurable lung metastases

Costs / coverage: 🟡 Partially funded. Study provides the oral drugs and tumor vaccines and covers nearly all study costs; limited specified bloodwork/supportive medications remain owner-paid.
