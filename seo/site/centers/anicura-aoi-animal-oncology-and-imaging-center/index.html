Cancer treatment & research options

Intralesional immunocytokine therapy for canine superficial tumors

AniCura AOI Animal Oncology and Imaging Center / ETH Zürich · Switzerland

Recruiting / participation invited on current AniCura AOI clinical-study page

What is being offered: Current AniCura AOI page states that dogs with superficial tumors are treated in a clinical study with intralesional special immunocytokines in collaboration with ETH Zürich. ETH research material describes canine F8/L19 immunocytokines and an initiated small clinical trial in dog patients with superficial tumors. Exact histologies and full eligibility require investigator prescreening.

Who may qualify: Active tumor required; Superficial accessible tumor

Costs / coverage: Confirm study-covered and owner-paid costs directly with AniCura AOI.

Contact: AniCura AOI — aoi@anicura.ch; +41 (0)41 783 07 77
