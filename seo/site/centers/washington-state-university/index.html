About WSU Veterinary Oncology

Washington State University's Veterinary Teaching Hospital provides multidisciplinary cancer care with chemotherapy, surgery and radiation therapy and recruits veterinary patients for clinical studies through its Oncology Service.

Current WSU oncology research includes local immunotherapy for canine soft tissue sarcoma using a hydrogel and T-cell engager approach. The clinical-studies team coordinates eligibility, treatment visits and study-supported care with the hospital's oncology and surgical services.

Cancer treatment & research options

Local T-cell engager immunotherapy before surgery for canine soft tissue sarcoma

Multicenter local T-cell-engager STS immunotherapy · USA

Current recruiting clinical study

What is being offered: Easily accessible STS >=2 cm and suitable surgical candidate. Hydrogel + T-cell engager injection 7 days before surgery. Study pays $1,000 toward WSU bill plus ICU, study-related medications and study bloodwork; owner pays standard-of-care surgery/diagnostics.

Who may qualify: Confirmed; Measurable; Planned surgery

Costs / coverage: AVMA does not specify funding for this protocol; do not display a funded badge unless a current participating-site source provides explicit cost coverage.
