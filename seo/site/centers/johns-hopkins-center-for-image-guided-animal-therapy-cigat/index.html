Cryotherapy or intratumoral immunotherapy before amputation for canine osteosarcoma

Johns Hopkins Center for Image-Guided Animal Therapy (CIGAT) · USA

Open and recruiting — listed on the current Johns Hopkins CIGAT clinical-trials page

What is being offered: Treatment trial for histopathologically confirmed canine osteosarcoma. Eligible dogs receive PET-CT and either cryotherapy or two doses of tumor-injected immunotherapy before required amputation at about two weeks, followed by chemotherapy. Johns Hopkins lists no prior osteosarcoma treatment or chemotherapy for other cancers and requires the dog to be systemically healthy apart from osteosarcoma.

Who may qualify: Confirmed; Active tumor required; Planned amputation and chemo

May not qualify if: Prior chemo; Prior osteosarcoma treatment

Costs / coverage: Study covers PET-CT/CT imaging, cryotherapy or intratumoral immunotherapy, study-related medications, and follow-up imaging/blood tests. Required amputation and chemotherapy are not covered by the study.

Contact: Johns Hopkins CIGAT — 410-502-7325; contact through the official clinical-trials page

Radiation therapy plus temozolomide for canine brain tumors

Johns Hopkins Center for Image-Guided Animal Therapy (CIGAT) · USA

Open and enrolling — current Johns Hopkins CIGAT clinical-trials page

What is being offered: Treatment study for dogs with a suspected glioma/intra-axial brain tumor greater than 1 cm on MRI and restricted to one side of the brain. Protocol uses five consecutive workdays of radiation therapy with concurrent oral temozolomide. Previous or current oncologic treatment of the brain tumor is exclusionary; investigator prescreening is required.

Who may qualify: Active tumor required; Min age years: 1; Min weight kg: 10; Brain tumor present

May not qualify if: Prior local radiation; Prior chemo

Costs / coverage: Radiation therapy and chemotherapy are fully funded. Study-related medications for side effects, follow-up MRI, and blood tests are also covered; treatment after progression is not covered.

Contact: Johns Hopkins CIGAT — 410-502-7325; contact through the official clinical-trials page

Kubanda veterinary cryotherapy for canine mammary cancer

Johns Hopkins Center for Image-Guided Animal Therapy (CIGAT) / Kubanda Cryotherapy · USA

Now recruiting — Phase IB trial listed on the current Johns Hopkins CIGAT clinical-trials page

What is being offered: Phase IB anticancer treatment trial of a veterinary cryotherapy device for dogs with mammary (breast) cancer. The current Johns Hopkins page explicitly states that the trial is recruiting; detailed public eligibility criteria are not posted, so investigator prescreening is required.

Who may qualify: Active tumor required

Costs / coverage: Confirm study-covered and owner-paid costs with the study team.

Contact: Kubanda Cryotherapy / Johns Hopkins CIGAT — contact through the official clinical-trials page
