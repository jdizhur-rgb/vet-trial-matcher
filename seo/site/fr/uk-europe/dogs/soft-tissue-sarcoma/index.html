FLASH radiotherapy for canine superficial malignant tumors

University Hospital for Companion Animals, University of Copenhagen / Lund University · Denmark

Recruiting/current project — University of Copenhagen lists patients for the 2020–2027 FLASH radiotherapy project

Treatment: Dogs with a cancer mass in skin, subcutis or oral cavity, including incompletely removed/residual tumor, may participate when there is expected therapeutic benefit. Dogs receive 1–4 radiotherapy treatments over up to four weeks; most receive FLASH and a smaller group conventional radiotherapy. Treatment requires travel within the Copenhagen/Lund collaboration.

Key eligibility: active_treatment_target: True; superficial_or_oral_tumor: True; anesthesia_fit: True

Funding / cost: The project states that treatment and follow-up visits are paid by the project; owner is responsible for EU pet passport/rabies requirements for travel to Sweden.
