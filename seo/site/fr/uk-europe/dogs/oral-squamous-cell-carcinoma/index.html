Intratumoral immunotherapy for canine oral malignant tumors

VetAgro Sup — Service of Cancerology · France

Ongoing European oncology clinical trial — listed by ESVONC/Oncowaf

What is being offered: Intratumoral immunotherapy for stage I–III oral melanoma or oral SCC with measurable disease at least 1 cm. Oncowaf lists no distant metastases and no prior treatment beyond surgical resection among key criteria.

Who may qualify: Confirmed; Active tumor required; Measurable; Min weight kg: 5; No metastasis

May not qualify if: Current steroids; Prior chemo; Prior local radiation; Prior immunotherapy

Costs / coverage: Confirm study-covered and owner-paid costs with VetAgro Sup.

Contact: VetAgro Sup Oncology — onco.chuvac@vetagro-sup.fr

Location

  • Centre hospitalier universitaire vétérinaire des animaux de compagnie, 1 avenue Bourgelat, 69280 Marcy-l'Étoile, France
