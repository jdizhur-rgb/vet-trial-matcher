Intratumoral immunotherapy for canine oral malignant tumors

VetAgro Sup — Service of Cancerology · France

Ongoing European oncology clinical trial — listed by ESVONC/Oncowaf

Treatment: Intratumoral immunotherapy for stage I–III oral melanoma or oral SCC with measurable disease at least 1 cm. Oncowaf lists no distant metastases and no prior treatment beyond surgical resection among key criteria.

Key eligibility: Confirmed; Active tumor required; Measurable; Min weight kg: 5; No metastasis

Important exclusions: Current steroids; Prior chemo; Prior local radiation; Prior immunotherapy

Costs: Confirm study-covered and owner-paid costs with VetAgro Sup.

Contact: VetAgro Sup Oncology — onco.chuvac@vetagro-sup.fr

Autologous hydroxyapatite-based cancer vaccine for canine mucosal melanoma

AniCura I Portoni Rossi / University of Teramo · Italy

Ongoing European oncology clinical trial — listed by Oncowaf

Treatment: Autologous hydroxyapatite-based cancer vaccine study for mucosal melanoma. The primary tumor must still be available for vaccine preparation; previously excised/formalin-fixed-only tumors are not eligible.

Key eligibility: Confirmed; Active tumor required

Costs: Confirm study-covered and owner-paid costs with the investigators.

Contact: Riccardo Finotello — riccardo.finotello@anicura.it; Valentina Rinaldi — vrinaldi@unite.it
