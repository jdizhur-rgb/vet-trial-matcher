Chemodectoma: Clinical Trials and Cancer Treatment Studies for Dogs in UK & Europe

About Chemodectoma

Chemodectoma, also called aortic body tumor, usually arises at the heart base. Some tumors grow slowly, while others cause cardiac compression, pericardial effusion or metastasis.

Standard treatment

Management depends on size, symptoms and resectability. Surgery is difficult for many heart-base tumors; radiation, including stereotactic approaches, may provide local control. Medical or targeted therapy and procedures for pericardial effusion may be used in selected patients.
