About Nasal Tumor

Nasal tumors arise within the nasal cavity or sinuses and are usually more threatening because of local invasion than early distant spread. Common signs include persistent nasal discharge, nosebleeds, noisy breathing or facial changes. The exact tumor type still matters because several cancers can occur in this location.

How it is usually treated

Radiation therapy is the main definitive local treatment for many canine and feline nasal cancers because complete surgery is rarely practical. Both conventionally fractionated and stereotactic radiation are used. Chemotherapy or targeted treatment may be added for selected tumor types or more advanced disease.

What can affect treatment choices

Pathology, extent within the nasal cavity and surrounding structures, lymph-node or distant spread, previous radiation, breathing symptoms and the specific tumor type guide treatment choices.
