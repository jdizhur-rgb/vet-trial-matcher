Microwave or radiofrequency thermoablation for canine hepatocellular carcinoma

AniCura Atlântico Hospital Veterinário · Portugal

Ongoing European oncology clinical trial — listed by Oncowaf

Treatment: Microwave or radiofrequency thermoablation for primary hepatocellular carcinoma or limited hepatic metastases; public criteria specify 1–6 cm lesions, normal hepatic function, no coagulopathy and ability to undergo anesthesia.
