Tumor Types Enrolling

+ Gliomas
+ Other brain tumors possibly eligible

Inclusion Criteria

- Must have a confirmed primary/recurrent
glioma or other eligible brain tumor

- Must be able to receive prednisone to
taper over 6 weeks for course of treatment

Exclusion Criteria

+ Severe chronic diseases (e.g. some
cardiovascular diseases, history of
anesthetic complications) that increase
the risk for anesthetic or treatment
complications

Patient is taking antioxidant supplements
that cannot be stopped

Patient has received radiation therapy in
the past for current cancer

Patient is receiving other therapies, such
as chemotherapy and immunotherapies,
and will not undergo a treatment
washout period/stop treatment

Procedures

+ Questionnaire before consultation, and
during each planned treatment and post-
treatment visit. Patients may receive
follow-up post-treatment visits at 2 and 4
weeks, 3 months, and then every 3
months for 1 year thereafter.

Physical exams at consultation,
treatment, and post-treatment visits
Sample collection (e.g. blood, urine,
feces, etc.) and possible imaging (MRI,
CT, X-ray, ultrasound, etc.) at
consultation, treatment, and post-
treatment visits. If patient is enrolled into
this study, a CT will be performed before
delivering radiation therapy. At least 2
MRIs will be performed throughout study.

*Clinicians may decide that your pet is in
need of additional samples/diagnostics at
any time throughout the study based on their
current health condition. Post-treatment
follow-up visits for 3 months are mandatory
for on-study pets.

Study #6

Veterinary Clinical Trials in Canine and
Feline Patients Using Combination Novel
Therapeutics, Devices, and Data
Collection Techniques

Study objective: To evaluate the combination of CAR neutrophils with
radiation in pet dogs with gliomas to induce tumor shrinkage and
improve overall survival by overcoming acquired therapeutic resistance
associated with treating brain tumors.

Treatments

+ Radiation therapy: delivered to the tumor over 1 week, a few
weeks, or a few months; treatment timeline varies on which arm
the patient gets assigned to

+ Immunotherapy: no treatment, weekly (2-4 weeks), or monthly
treatments; treatment timeline varies on which arm the patient
gets assigned to

Treatment Timeline

Referring vet or client contacts VROC and
sends patient’s medical records.

VROC clinicians review patient’s medical records and
decide if they may be eligible for a clinical trial.

Potentially eligible patients are scheduled for a consultation
and consented to the Master protocol for tissue and data
collection to confirm cancer and assess their health.

After consultation, clinicians review the patient’s case and
determine which treatment arm, if any, they qualify for.

The clinician will discuss treatment options with owner
and owner can consent to start determined treatment plan
for the patient’s cancer.

This study is subsidized. Owners will only pay for diagnostics, imaging, palliative care, etc. needed for off-study tests and care.
