Patient Disease: Canine appendicular osteosarcoma.

Study Title: Investigating the immunological effects and safety of integrating stereotactic body
radiation therapy and dendritic cell homing hydrogels for the treatment of canine osteosarcoma
(OSA).

Purpose of Study/Background/Objectives: Dendritic cell (DC) homing gels have been shown
to be safe and effective at stimulating local immune cells at the injection site. In this trial we will
be using stereotactic body radiation therapy (SBRT) along with DC-homing materials in dogs
with OSA. We aim to elicit immunogenic cell death of OSA cells with SBRT and integrate with
DC-homing materials for the development of potent in situ cancer vaccines. We will explore the
immunological effects and safety profile of integrating SBRT and DC-homing hydrogels for the
treatment of canine OSA. We are hopeful that combination therapy may be better at stimulating
the immune system against the primary tumor and the development of metastatic disease. In
this trial we will evaluate the combination to see if there is clinical benefit.

Inclusion Criteria:
    • Dogs > 1yr of age and at least 5 kg
    • Dogs with appendicular osteosarcoma that have a histologic or cytologic (alkaline
       phosphatase positive) diagnosis of sarcoma, consistent with osteosarcoma.
    • No metastatic disease on chest x-rays and abdominal ultrasound
    • No prior radiation or chemotherapy
    • No previous immunotherapy. No use of Apoquel, steroids (topical or systemic) or
       immune modulators such as cyclosporine within 14 days.
    • Adequate organ function for sedation

Exclusion Criteria:
   • Metastatic disease (chest or abdomen)
   • Previous radiation or chemotherapy or immunotherapy
   • Significant preexisting renal, hepatic, or cardiac disease
   • History of auto immune disease

Eligibility Diagnostics: Within 14 days of starting trial
    • CBC, Chemistry Panel, U/A
    • Cytology or histopathologic diagnosis with alkaline phosphatase positivity
    • Cytology of regional lymph node if accessible
    • 3 view chest x-rays
    • Abdominal Ultrasound
Treatment/Protocol:
   • Patients will receive a CT scan under anesthesia with tumor biopsy
   • Patients will receive radiation therapy 3 days in a row followed by hydrogel injection near
      the tumor site immediately after the 3rd radiation dose
   • Post treatment blood will be collected 4 days after radiation completion and then on
      weekly scheduled visits
   • After radiation, rechecks will be once a week for 5 weeks and then study is complete
   • Following completion of trial, patients may go on to other traditional treatments
