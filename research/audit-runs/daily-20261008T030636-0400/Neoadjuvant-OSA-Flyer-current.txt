CLINCIAL TRIAL FOR DOGS
WITH NEWLY DIAGNOSED
OSTEOSARCOMA
Patients will need to
travel to the University of
Pennsylvania for all visits
This clinical trial is investigating the ability of
genetically engineered CAR-iNKT cells from healthy
donor dogs to treat osteosarcoma and delay
metastatic disease in newly diagnosed dogs.
This clinical trial is fully funded and includes
Bone biopsy to diagnose osteosarcoma
CAR-iNKT cellular therapy
Amputation of affected limb
Standard of care chemotherapy after amputation
Regular follow up including labwork and chest radiographs
