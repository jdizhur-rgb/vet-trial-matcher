L45:   * 【13†Oncology Clinical Trials】
L46: Oncologists at the MSU Veterinary Medical Center conduct clinical research in order to find safer or more effective methods to screen for, prevent, diagnose, or treat cancers. Some findings may even have implications for human medicine.
L47: Clinical trials often require patients to meet certain requirements in order for the trial to effectively pinpoint its research investigations. Some trials offer subsidies or funding for care, and each is carefully designed and reviewed for safety. Please ask your veterinarian to contact MSU for further information about any of the studies listed here.
