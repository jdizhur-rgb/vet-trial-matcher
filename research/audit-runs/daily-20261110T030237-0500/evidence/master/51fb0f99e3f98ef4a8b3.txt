Clinical trials at The Ohio State University Veterinary Medical Center are funded by a variety of sponsors, including university researchers, foundations, and industry partners. Opportunities for participation change throughout the year and may be available for pets with a variety of health conditions. Clinical trials managed by BBVCTO are independent of the Blue Buffalo company and do not imply the use or evaluation of Blue Buffalo products unless specifically stated in an individual study. Interested in participating? Browse our currently enrolling studies or complete the Pre-Screening Questionnaire to determine whether your pet may qualify for an active clinical trial.
