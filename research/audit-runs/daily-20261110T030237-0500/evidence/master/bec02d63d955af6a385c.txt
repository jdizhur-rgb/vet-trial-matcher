Biospecimen Help build clinically relevant models of companion-animal cancer We partner with veterinary centres to develop patient-derived models from surplus, owner-consented samples collected during routine clinical care. Our initial areas of interest include melanoma, osteosarcoma, lymphoma and hemangiosarcoma. These collaborations support more clinically relevant drug development, comparative oncology research and future veterinary clinical studies. Clinical trials Become part of our future clinical research network We are building relationships with veterinary referral centres and oncology specialists interested in supporting future field studies. Our initial focus is expected to include canine osteosarcoma and lymphoma once our programmes are ready to progress into the clinic. Comparative oncology collaborations Advance cancer research across species We collaborate with veterinary clinicians and academic researchers interested in cancers that affect both companion animals and people. Projects may include patient-derived models, drug evaluation, biomarkers, tumour biology, translational research and future clinical studies. One disease. Two species. Shared progress. FAQs Is ValiRx Animal Health currently recruiting pets into clinical trials? + No. We will share details through our website, social media and participating veterinary centres when studies become available. How could my pet be considered for a future trial? + Recruitment would take place through participating veterinary hospitals, with eligibility assessed by the treating veterinary team. Could my pet contribute a sample for research? + In some cases, surplus material left after a necessary diagnostic or treatment procedure may be suitable for research. Can you advise me about my pet's treatment? + We cannot provide individual veterinary advice. Please speak to your veterinary surgeon or veterinary oncologist about diagnosis and treatment. How can I support your work? + You can follow our progress on our website and LinkedIn page. Share our work and help raise awareness of comparative oncology. FAQs What types of assets are you interested in? + We are interested in oncology drug candidates, biologics and technologies with a strong rationale for companion-animal cancer. At what stage will you consider an opportunity? + We will consider assets at different stages, provided there is enough evidence to support the mechanism and potential veterinary application. What information should I provide initially? + A short non-confidential summary covering the mechanism, target indication, available data, development status and partnering opportunity is usually sufficient. What can ValiRx Animal Health contribute? + We can contribute comparative oncology strategy, patient-derived model development, translational testing, programme management and veterinary partnership development. What types of co-development structure are possible? + Partnerships can be tailored to the opportunity and may include licences, option agreements, shared development programmes, milestones and downstream commercial rights. How do I invest in ValiRx Animal Health? + Please contact us to arrange a confidential discussion about current investment opportunities.
