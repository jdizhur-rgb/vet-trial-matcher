Health Simulation <a id=\"adelaide-health-simulation\"></a>Adelaide Health Simulation (AHS) is the only healthcare simulation facility in Australia holding dual accreditation from the Society for Simulation in Healthcare, recognised internationally for teaching, education, and research.</p>\r\n<p>Located within Adelaide University's Adelaide Health and Medical Sciences building, AHS is a multi-floor, purpose-built centre training health professions students, clinicians, and healthcare organisations across the full arc of care — from birth through to end of life. Located within Adelaide University's Adelaide Health and Medical Sciences building, AHS is a multi-floor, purpose-built centre training health professions students, clinicians, and healthcare organisations across the full arc of care — from birth through to end of life. Clinical simulation suites mirror real hospital environments, with functioning oxygen and suction systems, nurse call, and an integrated electronic medical record and equipment aligned to local health networks. Twenty-four dedicated assessment rooms provide full audiovisual capture for regulatory-level clinical assessment. AHS runs a world-leading simulated patient program, delivered in partnership with the State Theatre Company of South Australia - a partnership unique in Australian clinical education. A professor-led team of academics, simulation educators, and simulation technicians supports clinical education, professional development, Higher Degree by Research programs including PhDs, and active international collaborations - advancing the evidence base for simulation in healthcare worldwide.
