Each listing below has a quick summary plus a link to more information for each study. Species Dog Category Dermatology Current Status Open and enrolling Canine Brain Tumor Program Leading edge research combined with compassionate care Species Dog Category Neurology Current Status Open and enrolling CAR-T cell therapy for canines with B-cell lymphoma A canine clinical trial designed to evaluate the safety and efficacy of a CAR-T cell therapy product for the treatment B-cell lymphoma Species Dog Category Cancer/Oncology Current Status Open and enrolling COED part 1 - dogs with Osteosarcoma ages 2-4 Blood sampling study of dogs to develop test for early detection of canine osteosarcoma. Species Dog Category Cancer/Oncology Current Status Ongoing but enrollment closed COED part 2 - canine Osteosarcoma Study to develop a reliable, minimally-invasive blood test for early detection of canine osteosarcoma. Species Dog Category Cancer/Oncology Current Status Ongoing but enrollment closed FEHI - Feline SEHI for OA Study for cats with radiographic evidence of osteoarthritis using a novel painkiller Species Cat Category Anesthesia and pain management Current Status Open and enrolling Genetic muscle diseases in horses Species Horse Category Genetics Internal medicine Current Status Open and enrolling Lyra 1 - blood collection study for lymphoma A minimally invasive blood sampling study to identify dogs at high risk of developing Diffuse Large B-cell Lymphoma (DLBCL). Samples must be collected at the University of Minnesota, Small Animal Hospital. Species Dog Category Cancer/Oncology Current Status Ongoing but enrollment closed LYRA 3 - blood collection study for lymphoma in healthy dogs at least 5 years old This project aims to create a simple blood test that can accurately check if a healthy dog has undetected diffuse large b-cell lymphoma (DLBCL) or is at high risk of developing it. In the future, this test could be paired with targeted treatments to prevent tumors from developing. Species Dog Category Cancer/Oncology Current Status Open and enrolling New vaccine for dogs with oral melanoma Novel adeno-associated virus (AAV) vaccine for dogs with malignant oral melanoma Species Dog Category Cancer/Oncology Current Status Open and enrolling Olaparib + Meloxicam for Feline Mammary Carcinoma A pilot clinical trial to evaluate the safety and efficacy of combination olaparib and meloxicam therapy for treatment of feline mammary carcinoma Species Cat Category Cancer/Oncology Current Status Open and enrolling ORBIT - VSV and an anti-PD-1 inhibitor for Canine Lymphoma This study hopes to understand how a new combination immunotherapy works and to determine if it is effective in the treatment for dogs with relapsed and refractory aggressive lymphoma. Species Dog Category Cancer/Oncology Current Status Open and enrolling Puppy Gait Analysis Study A canine clinical trial to evaluate gait development and coordination in puppies into adulthood.
