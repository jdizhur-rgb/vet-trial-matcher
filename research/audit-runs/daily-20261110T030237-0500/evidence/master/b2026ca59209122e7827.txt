Clinical trials for dogs Clinical trials for cats Clinical trials for horses What are veterinary clinical trials? Veterinary clinical trials are research studies involving animals. They evaluate treatments, diagnostic tests, medical devices, or procedures to answer questions about animal health. Participation does not guarantee a benefit for an individual animal. Discuss a study with your veterinarian and the research team before deciding whether to take part. Ask about eligibility, possible risks, costs, and required visits. Cornell's clinical trial questions and answers explain what to consider. How to search VetTrials Start with a species above or type a condition or treatment into the search box. For example, search for lymphoma . Narrow the results by condition, state, institution, or listed enrollment status. On a phone, tap Show Filters. You can also browse dog oncology trials , cat kidney trials , or horse trials . Open a listing and follow its institution link.
