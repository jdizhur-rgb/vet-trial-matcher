Register your interest → See how it works → Why we are running this trial From Rosie’s story to a future for more dogs. Rosie The first dog treated with a personalised neoantigen vaccine. It began with Rosie. Her experience with a personalised neoantigen vaccine encouraged Paul to explore how this approach could one day be made available to every dog with cancer. Before an approach like this can become broadly available, we must study it carefully and support it by evidence for review by the relevant veterinary regulators. This clinical trial is an important step in that process, helping us learn from participating dogs and understand the vaccine more fully. Register your interest → About the trial What this trial is looking at The trial will investigate a personalised mRNA vaccine approach for dogs with mast cell cancer. Tell us about your dog and our team will help determine whether they may be eligible for screening. Who can register Dogs diagnosed with mast cell cancer who are based in, or able to travel within, Australia. Every case is reviewed individually The trial approach A therapeutic mRNA vaccine designed from each dog’s own tumour and blood samples, intended to work alongside—not replace—existing care. Personalised to each dog What screening involves We help coordinate records, screening, sample logistics and updates, working with the trial team and your treating veterinarian.
