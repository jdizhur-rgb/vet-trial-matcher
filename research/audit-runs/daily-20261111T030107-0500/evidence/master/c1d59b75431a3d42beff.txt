Current clinical trials and translational research studies at VMCVM --> General Item Evaluation of a treatment for Equine Osteoarthritis The purpose of this study is to assess the effect of BIO-PLY on lameness reduction in horses affected with osteoarthritis. tag: Horses tag: Recruiting --> --> General Item A study of Paroxetine in Horses The purpose of this study is to evaluate the pharmacokinetics and clinical efficacy of paroxetine, a human antidepressant, in horses after single and 30-day doses. By finding a safe and effective dosage, this research aims to improve management and quality of life for horses displaying stereotypic behaviors like cribbing, wind sucking, and weaving --> --> General Item Non-invasive treatment of Equine melanomas The purpose of this study is to evaluate the effectiveness of using histotripsy, a non-invasive treatment, to treat melanomas in horses and measure its impact on the immune system. tag: Oncology tag: Horses tag: Recruiting --> --> General Item A Non-Invasive Approach to treating Equine Sarcoids Purpose: To evaluate the effectiveness of histotripsy as a non-invasive therapeutic approach for treating equine sarcoids. tag: Surgery tag: Horses --> --> General Item Liver tumor PDT The purpose of this study is to test a new treatment called photodynamic therapy (PDT) for dogs who've been diagnosed with liver tumors. The goal is to find out if PDT can be done safely, and see if it helps destroy the tumor cells in the liver. The long term objective is to develop a minimally invasive way of treating liver tumors. tag: Oncology tag: Recruiting tag: Dogs --> --> General Item Developing a non-invasive treatment for canine thyroid tumors The purpose of the study is to evaluate histotripsy, a new, non-invasive way of treating canine thyroid tumors that can precisely target and destroy abnormal tissues without incisions or radiation. tag: Oncology tag: Recruiting tag: Dogs --> --> General Item Evaluating a Clotting Enzyme in Dogs with Protein Losing Nephropathy The purpose of the study is to evaluate a clotting enzyme, ADAMTS13, in dogs with protein losing nephropathy (PLN). Protein losing nephropathy is a form of kidney disease that causes protein loss in the urine. Dogs with PLN have a higher risk of blood clotting disorders, but the mechanism isn’t well understood. tag: Internal Medicine tag: Recruiting tag: Dogs --> --> General Item Comparing measurements in dogs with Pulmonary Hypertension The purpose of this study is to compare the accuracy of measurements in the lungs obtained from a non-invasive heart ultrasound (echocardiogram) to the measurements obtained from right heart catheterization to better diagnose, classify, and treat pulmonary hypertension in dogs. tag: Cardiology tag: Recruiting tag: Dogs --> --> General Item New Therapy for Canine Brain Tumors Purpose: To determine if it is safe and feasible to use a light-sensitive drug called verteporfin, in combination with photodynamic therapy (PDT), in dogs with newly diagnosed brain tumors. tag: Neurology tag: Oncology tag: Recruiting tag: Dogs --> --> General Item Evaluating Effects of a new Bone Tumor Treatment Purpose: To evaluate the effects of histotripsy, a novel treatment for that can destroy tissue without radiation or heat, on dogs with osteosarcoma. tag: Oncology tag: Recruiting tag: Dogs --> --> General Item Amputation and Chemotherapy for Osteosarcoma We are seeking dogs whose owners have decided on the standard of care treatment option for canine osteosarcoma. This includes amputation and chemotherapy. This study aims to check for the spread (metastasis) of the cancer and will follow patients until the time which the dog develops metastasis. We will use this information to compare against our other clinical trials. tag: Oncology tag: Recruiting tag: Dogs --> --> General Item A Nerve Conduction study on Dogs with Intervertebral Disc Extrusion Purpse: To determine if a nerve test, performed on paralyzed dogs before surgery for a slipped disc, can predict whether they will regain the ability to walk. tag: Neurology tag: Recruiting tag: Dogs --> --> General Item Canine Lipomas Purpose: To evaluate the safety and effectiveness of treating lipomas in dogs with a single treatment. tag: Community Practice tag: Recruiting tag: Dogs
