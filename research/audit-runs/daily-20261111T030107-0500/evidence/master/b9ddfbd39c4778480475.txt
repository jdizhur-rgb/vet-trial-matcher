Trials help answer questions, and the answers can benefit both animal and human health because of the many diseases common to both. By participating in a clinical trial, you and your pet are helping us advance the future health and well-being of animals and humans. The Lloyd Veterinary Medical Center has outstanding veterinary expertise through its board-certified clinicians, faculty, and technical staff in a state-of-the-art facility. We have expertise and experience in conducting clinical trials and analyzing the data.
