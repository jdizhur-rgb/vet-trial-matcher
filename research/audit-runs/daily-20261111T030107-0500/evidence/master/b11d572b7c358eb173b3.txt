Advancing the future of veterinary cancer care. 🐾 In collaboration with UConn Cancer Institute, Calusa is participating in an ongoing study exploring an innovative immunotherapeutic approach to canine lymphoma. Our very own Dr. Turkell is the only doctor in the world currently administering this treatment. This investigational therapy uses an antisense oligonucleotide (ASO) designed to help the immune system recognize and attack cancer cells. As part of the study, this patient's treatment is provided at no cost.
