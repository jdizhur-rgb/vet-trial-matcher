Hemangiosarcoma is one of the most aggressive cancers a dog can face. After surgery, vets usually turn to doxorubicin, a human chemotherapy that is not FDA-approved for use in dogs. It helps, but it strains the heart with every dose, so most dogs can only receive limited treatments before they have to stop, even when the cancer hasn't. FAM-010 is designed to be gentler on the heart, so treatment can go further FAM-010 is a new form of doxorubicin. It is attached to albumin, a natural protein in the blood, which is designed to carry the medicine to the tumor and release it there. Because less of the medicine reaches the rest of the body, we expect FAM-010 to be less toxic than standard doxorubicin, especially to the heart. We are studying whether more medicine reaching the tumor, for a longer period of time, can potentially mean better control of the cancer and more time for dogs with the people who love them.
