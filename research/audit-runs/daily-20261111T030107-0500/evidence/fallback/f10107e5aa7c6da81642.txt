Through a carefully orchestrated process, we collaborate with pharmaceutical companies and regulatory bodies worldwide to secure required approvals and ensure the safe and ethical distribution of these therapies. Veterinarians gain exclusive access to these cutting-edge drugs, biologics, and devices through a structured application process, where VMD Sciences handles the regulatory complexities, including export certificates, import permits, and customs inquiries, allowing vets to administer treatments under controlled conditions, even before full market approval. For veterinarians , VMAPs offer significant benefits by enabling them to provide critical care to their patients when standard options are insufficient, enhancing their ability to manage complex cases and improve animal outcomes. This access empowers vets to stay at the forefront of veterinary medicine, offering hope to pet owners and building trust in their practice, while also providing hands-on experience with emerging therapies that can inform future treatment protocols. For small and large Pharmaceutical and biotechnology companies , VMAPs are a strategic opportunity to introduce their products to the market early, gathering invaluable Real-World Data (RWD) to support regulatory submissions and refine development strategies. VMD Sciences guides pharma partners from start to finish, overseeing the entire VMAP lifecycle, including protocol design, patient enrollment, data collection, and post-program regulatory submission support, ensuring a robust evidence base that accelerates approvals and enhances market positioning. Our comprehensive approach ensures that veterinarians receive the tools they need to excel, while pharmaceutical companies benefit from actionable insights and a smoother path to commercialization. By leveraging our global expertise, VMD Sciences not only facilitates access, but also fosters a collaborative ecosystem that advances animal health worldwide, making MAPs a cornerstone of our commitment to innovation and excellence in veterinary care. We recognize that animal drug shortages can create significant challenges for veterinary professionals and patient care.
