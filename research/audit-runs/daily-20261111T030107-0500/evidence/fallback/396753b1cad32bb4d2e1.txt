The therapy (GCN2-IN-6) has been carefully tested in preclinical studies and is well-tolerated. Your referred patients will receive standard surgical care plus the investigational therapy. Participation contributes valuable data for advancing cancer treatments in both cats and humans. Study design highlights Neo-adjuvant and adjuvant therapy administered around the time of mastectomy. Safety and efficacy are continuously monitored. Feline mammary adenocarcinoma is a validated model for TNBC research. Referral process Eligible cats: cats diagnosed with mammary tumors, eligible for surgery. As referring veterinarians, you can retain primary responsibility for routine care; the study team coordinates treatment administration and follow-up. Participation is voluntary, and owners can withdraw at any time. Impact: By referring cats to this study, you are helping advance cutting-edge cancer research, improve therapeutic options, and potentially enhance outcomes for both feline and human patients.
