## New entries and primary evidence checked

- CanCan K9-LiquiDX: https://cancandiagnostics.com/ and https://cancandiagnostics.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/CanCan-Diagnostics-K9-LiquiDX.pdf. Blood cfDNA sequencing; serial cancer monitoring, with variable sensitivity. UK laboratory and current kit ordering. USA shipping is documented in the existing CanCan article and prior direct correspondence; Europe includes the UK laboratory access. No Canadian availability inferred. No feline product added: website places it in a future pipeline.
- Oncept: https://animalhealth.boehringer-ingelheim.com/pets/canine/products/therapeutics/oncept and https://www.aphis.usda.gov/sites/default/files/2024-09/124-9240d0.pdf. Human tyrosinase DNA vaccine, stage II/III oral melanoma after control of primary tumor and affected nodes. Mixed survival evidence: comparative retrospective study https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23909996/ found no survival improvement; treated cohort https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32323424/ does not establish comparative benefit.
- Laverdia: https://www.fda.gov/animal-veterinary/cvm-updates/fda-grants-full-approval-first-oral-treatment-lymphoma-dogs and https://www.dechra-us.com/our-products/us/companion-animal/dog/prescription/laverdia-verdinexor-tablets. Full approval January 16, 2026, oral canine lymphoma treatment; XPO1 inhibition, veterinary monitoring and handling requirements. No CHOP-equivalence claim.
- Tanovea: https://my.elanco.com/us/tanovea and https://animaldrugsatfda.fda.gov/adafda/app/search/public/document/downloadFoi/11083 and https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=5b36e345-fae9-40ea-a069-6fefba92a89f. Fully approved July 15, 2021; rabacfosadine interferes with DNA synthesis. Retain potentially fatal pulmonary fibrosis warning and labeled contraindications. Do not call it new or repeat outdated manufacturer claim that it is the only fully approved canine lymphoma drug.
