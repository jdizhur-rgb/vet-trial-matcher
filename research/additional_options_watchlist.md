# Additional oncology options — watchlist and exclusions

Last reviewed: 2026-09-04

This file is internal research support for the patient-facing **Additional Options to Discuss With Your Oncologist** page. Items here are **not** patient-facing matches unless promoted after evidence + access review.

## Inclusion rule

A non-routine experimental/off-label/precision option needs meaningful comparative canine/feline clinical evidence with relevant outcomes and a real current access pathway in the USA or Europe. A regulated veterinary oncology therapy may pass without a comparator when it has prospective clinical efficacy data and a real specialist-access pathway. Do not promote options shown to perform worse than appropriate standard treatment. Do not promote case reports, tiny uncontrolled responder series, mechanistic rationale, preclinical work, or commercial availability alone.

Geography of discovery is irrelevant. Research from Brazil, Argentina, Mexico, or elsewhere may lead to a card only if the resulting therapy/test can actually be obtained, coordinated, referred to, or implemented in the USA or Europe. Do not add new country markets merely because research was found there.

## Deep regional audit — Poland / Czechia / Austria

### Poland
- **SGGW Warsaw gold-nanoparticle nano-oncology — canine osteosarcoma / feline injection-site sarcoma** — ACTIVE RESEARCH LEAD, not currently eligible for patient-facing Additional Options. The Laboratory of Experimental and Clinical NanoOncology currently lists gold nanoparticles for canine OSA metastasis, targeted AuNP therapy for canine OSA/FISS, and photothermal AuNP therapy for FISS. It collaborates with a Warsaw veterinary oncology clinic and, since 2024, a substances/pharmaceuticals research company. The laboratory describes its goal as introduction of personalized animal-cancer therapy, but the clinical translation is not yet demonstrated.
- **Evidence check:** canine OSA Au-GSH work located in 2021, 2023 and 2025 remains in-vitro/CAM/ex-ovo; the 2025 paper explicitly says further in-vivo work is required to assess clinical relevance. FISS Au-GSH-doxorubicin work located is chick-embryo and murine/preclinical. No comparative client-owned dog/cat outcomes found.
- **PEG-liposomal doxorubicin for metastatic canine OSA** — SGGW 2023 work is in-vitro/ex-ovo, not a client-owned dog treatment study. Do not promote as a Polish trial or Additional Option from this evidence.
- **International Centre for Cancer Vaccine Science (University of Gdańsk) / Edinburgh comparative canine immunotherapy** — HIGH-VALUE TRANSLATIONAL LEAD. ICCVS has an explicit companion-dog comparative-oncology strand, previously announced collaboration with Tri-City Veterinary Clinic to establish a Comparative Oncology Centre for canine patients, and current/very recent canine checkpoint-antibody work with Edinburgh and Brno. A 2024 paper characterized two canine PD-1 antibodies that block PD-1/PD-L1 and describes them as potential veterinary therapeutics. A 2025/2026 Brno–Gdańsk–Edinburgh paper generated canine TIM3 monoclonal antibodies that disrupt TIM3–Galectin-9; the authors note TIM3 has high pan-cancer expression in canine cancers. These are **preclinical/translational antibodies, not owner-accessible therapy and not a recruiting trial**. Monitor aggressively for first client-owned-dog dose-escalation/clinical protocol.
- **ICCVS PD-1/osteosarcoma project** — an ongoing-project listing documents a comparative-oncology project on PD-1 intrinsic signalling in osteosarcoma (2023–2026). This is mechanistic research, not evidence of treatment enrollment. Do not convert project/grant status into recruiting status.
- **ICCVS human SWIFT/CANVAS cancer-vaccine programs** — human oncology development; do not confuse with veterinary access. The dog work is useful as a technology pipeline only.
- **Wrocław University of Environmental and Life Sciences** — current curriculum and research infrastructure confirm veterinary experimental oncology, cancer pharmacotherapy, immunotherapy and cell-therapy expertise, but deep Polish/English searches did not verify an owner-facing recruiting anticancer treatment trial. Continue investigator-level monitoring rather than adding a generic Wrocław trial.
- **Other Polish veterinary schools (Lublin, Kraków) searched via institution, Polish and English disease/treatment terms:** oncology publications and tumor biology were found, but no current owner-facing recruiting anticancer treatment study meeting our treatment-only rules was verified in this audit.

### Czechia
- **Animed Brno / Jakub Pfeifr** — important clinical lead. The referral clinic explicitly provides veterinary oncology, chemotherapy and ECT, coordinates radiotherapy in Vienna/Postojna, uses molecular/genetic testing, collaborates with veterinary research institutes in Brno and Vienna, and states that selected canine melanoma patients can be provided biological treatment/vaccine. This is real clinical access, but the therapies described are services rather than a verified recruiting clinical trial.
- **Animed melanoma vaccine identity — likely ONCEPT pathway, not a novel Czech platform.** Czech small-animal oncology guidance explicitly identifies ONCEPT as the canine melanoma gene-therapy vaccine, and Czech regulatory records document historical exceptional import/use of ONCEPT. Vetmeduni Vienna also used ONCEPT in canine oral melanoma patients. Animed does not name the product on its current page, so identity is not formally proven, but the evidence now makes ONCEPT the leading explanation. **Do not create a novel Czech vaccine card or trial.** If exact identity matters, confirm directly with Animed before encoding.
- **ONCEPT evidence caution:** current U.S. manufacturer indication is stage II–III oral melanoma after local disease control, but a critical review of canine oral melanoma literature rates the overall vaccination evidence as low quality because of inconsistent controls/confounding. It is an established licensed adjunct rather than a Czech experimental treatment. Do not misrepresent Animed access as clinical-trial recruitment.
- **VETUNI Brno** — research/oncology infrastructure confirmed; historical joint lymphoma cohorts with Vetmeduni Vienna confirm substantial clinical caseload. No current owner-facing recruiting anticancer treatment study was verified through Czech/English searches in this pass.
- **Brno immunotherapy lead:** Jakub Pfeifr is publicly described as collaborating scientifically in oncology/immunotherapy with the Czech Academy of Sciences. Treat as an investigator lead only; no patient recruitment or qualifying treatment protocol was located yet.
- **Masaryk Memorial Cancer Institute Brno / RECAMO** — HIGH-VALUE TRANSLATIONAL LEAD connected to the same Gdańsk/Edinburgh canine checkpoint program. RECAMO authors co-developed/characterized canine PD-1 and TIM3 antibodies. Current evidence is antibody engineering/in-vitro functional blockade, not administration to Czech client-owned dogs. Monitor for veterinary translation, especially if Animed/VETUNI becomes a clinical partner.

### Austria
- **Vetmeduni Vienna** — active specialist oncology and radiation oncology; currently offers modern linear-accelerator RT and treats canine/feline skin, bone, oral, brain and nasal tumors. This is current clinical service, not by itself a clinical trial.
- **Stereotactic RT for canine brain tumors** — Vetmeduni implemented stereotactic radiation in Nov 2023 and a 2025 thesis analyzes the first treated cohort. This confirms current capability but not a recruiting experimental trial; RT is also a standard specialist modality, so do not create an Additional Options card solely from this.
- **Canine EGFR-targeted IgE immunotherapy** — Vetmeduni/MedUni Vienna published a preclinical canine anti-EGFR IgE program and announced a subsequent canine-patient clinical trial in 2018. Deep search did not verify a current 2026 recruiting trial, current access program, or qualifying clinical outcome dataset. Keep as a historical/translational lead, not a live trial.
- **Historical mammary carcinoma vaccine trial** — Vetmeduni previously recruited dogs with inoperable mammary carcinoma into a phase I/II tumor-vaccine study; the recruitment evidence located is from 2009 and cannot be treated as current.
- **c-KIT mutation testing for canine MCT** — current Vetmeduni assay/service exists and was developed to inform TKI decisions. This is clinically actionable testing, but c-KIT/TKI management is established oncology practice and does not meet the non-routine Additional Options threshold by itself.
- **Comparative-oncology / Ludwig Boltzmann network lead** — infrastructure for pet-animal clinical trials/new-drug access has been publicly described, but no current owner-facing qualifying anticancer recruitment was verified in this audit.
- **Vienna melanoma treatment history confirms cross-border access** — a 2021 retrospective Vetmeduni cohort of 33 canine oral melanomas included 14 dogs treated with ONCEPT and one with PetBioCell in addition to RT. This confirms these therapies have actually reached Vienna patients, but it is historical treatment, not current recruitment and not proof of benefit.

## Watch — not patient-facing

- **SGGW Warsaw nano-oncology** — recheck specifically for first prospective client-owned canine OSA or feline FISS treatment protocol, ethics approval/recruitment notice, or clinical-access program. If found, route to Clinical Trials first; Additional Options only after evidence threshold is met.
- **Gdańsk ICCVS / Edinburgh / Brno canine PD-1 and TIM3 antibody pipeline** — strongest new Poland/Czechia research lead from this audit. Watch publications, grants, veterinary-clinic partnerships and recruitment pages for transition from antibody characterization to treatment of client-owned dogs. Do not infer recruitment from active grants.
- **Animed Brno canine melanoma biological/vaccine treatment** — ONCEPT is now the leading identity hypothesis; confirm product directly before encoding exact access. Do not treat as a new experimental Czech vaccine without contrary evidence.
- **Brno oncology/immunotherapy collaboration (Pfeifr / Czech Academy / RECAMO)** — investigator-level lead; search future publications, grants and recruitment notices for a client-owned treatment protocol.
- **Vetmeduni/MedUni anti-EGFR canine IgE** — historical planned canine trial; recheck only if a later clinical publication or active recruitment/access path appears.
- **OST-HER2, canine osteosarcoma** — comparative signal exists, but prior U.S. conditional-license access expired; reconsider only if U.S. clinical access/distribution is restored.
- **ECT + IL-2/IL-12 gene electrotransfer, canine oral melanoma (Europe)** — comparative PFS signal, but no clear OS advantage and current owner access needs confirmation. Do not promote yet.
- **CPMV intratumoral immunotherapy ± checkpoint blockade** — technology has been used by U.S. research groups, but current evidence is too small/uncontrolled for this page. A research collaboration/access pathway alone is not enough.
- **Argentine melanoma immunogene/cytokine platform** — large comparative clinical dataset is interesting, but no verified U.S./European implementation or material/technology-transfer access pathway. Do not send U.S. owners to Argentina as a routine result.

## Excluded from Additional Options

- **Histiocytic sarcoma / FidoCure** — do not show. Preliminary small comparative subgroup data are not strong enough for this page.
- **ELIAS ECI alone for canine osteosarcoma** — exclude because controlled field data did not establish benefit over appropriate carboplatin standard therapy and showed unfavorable survival comparison. Combination programs can be reconsidered only with mature comparative evidence.
- **Generic autologous tumor vaccines for solid tumors** — exclude as a class; evidence is heterogeneous and often uncontrolled. APAVAC/Vaxkit is included only for B-cell lymphoma, where a disease-specific comparative clinical cohort exists.
- **PetBioCell dendritic-cell therapy — do not promote generically.** It is currently commercially accessible in Europe and can coordinate blood collection/cell return through a local veterinarian, but access alone is insufficient. Crucially, a prospective randomized double-blind placebo-controlled study in 21 dogs with stage I–II splenic HSA found no significant tumor-free-interval or survival benefit (median survival 74 days PetBioCell vs 126 days placebo). This triggers our negative filter for splenic HSA. A later company-affiliated prospective HSA publication does not erase the randomized negative study. For other tumor types, require disease-specific qualifying comparative evidence before reconsideration.
- **Torigen autologous vaccine** — insufficient comparative efficacy evidence for patient-facing inclusion.
- **Brazil Multikinase / OncoTarget / OncoScan case-based precision recommendations** — case reports, target expression, or actionability are not enough to establish clinical benefit.
- **Brazil Quimiograma functional drug-sensitivity work** — research/validation stage; no qualifying comparative clinical outcome evidence/current routine access pathway.
- **VetFeron / MRB-CFI-1V** — uncontrolled/small clinical series do not meet the threshold; access is also research-limited.
- **Mexico CPMV + anti-PD-1 proof-of-concept** — very small uncontrolled cohort; technology lead only, not a treatment card.
- **Mexico HugPetmab anti-PD-1 proof-of-concept** — very small uncontrolled cohort; no qualifying comparator.
- **Single-case targeted therapy reports** (including Brazilian HS, GIST/lapatinib, pancreatic carcinoma/toceranib and similar reports) — never promote from a single case.

## Product-design rule

The owner-facing page should stay sparse. Zero qualifying additional options for a cancer is an acceptable and often preferable result. The purpose is to reduce noise for owners and oncologists, not to generate a long list of speculative things to ask about.
