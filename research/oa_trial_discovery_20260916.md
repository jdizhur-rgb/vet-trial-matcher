## Deduplication notes
1. Gallant feline OA is ONE sponsor protocol with multiple participating U.S. clinics. Do not create a trial per clinic.
2. Clinaxel CX25/011 is ONE CRO project until evidence shows distinct protocols.
3. WVRC vaccine is ONE protocol even if additional Ethos/NVA/private sites surface; link sites to the same trial by intervention/design/criteria/sponsor.
4. CANDO projects may appear simultaneously on network, university and private-practice pages; protocol identity must be resolved before counting.
5. Cornell PRP and Cornell radiofrequency are distinct interventions and remain separate.
6. Tennessee collar and Tennessee polymer-bead/HA hip study are distinct protocols despite overlapping investigators and eligibility fields.
7. Do not dedupe by title alone; use intervention + disease/joint + protocol/PI/sponsor + eligibility + site network.

## Matching-field schema emerging from verified trials
- species
- age / age range
- weight / size class
- confirmed OA vs owner-observed mobility signs
- radiographic OA required / obtained at screening
- affected joint: shoulder / elbow / hip / stifle / multi-joint
- OA cause/subtype: hip dysplasia, CCL-related, primary/other
- OA severity
- duration and stability of pain / lameness
- refractory or inadequately controlled pain
- owner-observed mobility impairment
- current analgesics: NSAID, gabapentin, other
- prior/current anti-NGF monoclonal antibody exposure (especially Librela)
- medication and supplement washout requirements
- prior intra-articular injections / PRP / stem cells / orthobiologics
- prior orthopedic surgery and timing
- joint instability / concurrent orthopedic disease
- neurologic disease affecting gait / IVDD
- immune-mediated disease / polyarthropathy
- clinically significant systemic comorbidity / abnormal labs
- pregnancy/lactation/breeding status when applicable
- temperament / ability to complete gait or behavioral testing
- indoor-only requirement for feline studies when applicable
- geographic/site requirement
- visit/follow-up burden
- treatment vs observational study

## Discovery and import rules
1. Aggregators, registry hits and search results are discovery signals only.
2. Prefer a current university, hospital, sponsor or CRO primary page before marking active.
3. A multicenter protocol is one trial with multiple sites, not multiple trials.
4. Match duplicates by intervention + condition + protocol/PI/sponsor + eligibility, not title alone.
5. Do not import closed, completed, stale or ambiguous historical pages as active.
6. Keep observational OA studies separate from treatment trials so a future matcher can default to treatment options.
7. “Coming up” is watchlist, not recruiting.
8. A live archive/tag page without explicit enrollment wording is insufficient to mark a protocol active.
9. Before future matcher import, recheck every ACTIVE record's status and capture exact sites/contact/funding fields.
